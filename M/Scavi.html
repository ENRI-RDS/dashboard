Guide — Works Progress Dashboard
📋 What this page shows

The dashboard tracks the excavation progress for the project's routes across all active lots. For each section the current worksite status, percentage of metres excavated, and any operational blockages are monitored.

At the top, the KPI cards show how many sections (and how many km) are in each phase. The lot bars show the overall excavation progress per lot. The table lists all sections with filters. The donut chart distributes total km by status.

📌 Status descriptions
Not StartedSection awaiting permit or mobilisation. No excavation activity has begun yet.
Setting UpWorksite being mobilised: machinery, fencing, and site logistics in preparation. Excavation has not yet started.
In ProgressActive excavation underway. The progress percentage indicates metres completed vs total section length.
On HoldExcavation temporarily suspended due to operational, logistical, or authorisation issues. Partial progress is retained.
🏆 CompletedExcavation finished for the entire section. The section is ready for the next construction phase.
💡 Quick tips

• Click a KPI card to see the list of sections in that status, filterable by lot.
• Click a lot bar to open the detail list for that lot.
• Click a table row to open the section detail.
• Use the search box and the status filter in the table to navigate quickly.
• The overall excavation progress (sidebar) also accounts for partial metres in sections that are in progress, setting up, or on hold.
